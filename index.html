Design is

My background spans product design, brand identity, and front-end prototyping. I've worked with startups and Fortune 500 companies across fintech, health, and cultural institutions.

How I Work

The design
process.

◎ 01 — Discover
Research & Listen

Deep user interviews, competitive analysis, and stakeholder alignment before a single pixel is placed.

◈ 02 — Define
Frame the Problem

Synthesising findings into sharp problem statements, personas, and measurable success criteria.

◇ 03 — Design
Craft & Prototype

From rough sketches to high-fidelity interactive prototypes — iterated with real users, not assumptions.

◉ 04 — Deliver
Ship & Measure

Developer handoff with design specs, component libraries, and post-launch analytics review.
